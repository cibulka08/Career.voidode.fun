Questions about applying.

Is Void hiring?

Yes. We're looking for developers, designers, moderators, community managers and support people for Void, the Discord bot by Voidone.

How do I apply?

Fill in the application form on this page. It has three short steps and takes about 10 minutes.

Do I need experience?

There are no strict requirements. Tell us what you can do, what you've made and what you'd like to learn. We care about how you think.

What happens after I apply?

The team reads your application and emails you the result with feedback on how you did. If it's a match, we invite you to a short voice interview on Discord.

Can I apply more than once?

You can send one application every 2 days. If you want to change something, wait for your reply or ask us on Discord.

Which languages do I need?

Applications are written in English. Tell us in the form which other languages you speak, because that helps us place you in the community.

Who owns the work I create for Void?

Everything you create for Void while you're on the team belongs to the Void project. You can read the terms before you submit your application.

What is Void?

Void is a Discord bot by Voidone for moderation, welcome messages, levels and logging. It is in beta. Read more about the Void bot.

Where can I ask questions before applying?

In the support server on Discord. The team answers questions about the roles and the process there.
